PROJECT NO. PR-2-5-1 — NATIONAL HOUSING AGENCY
NO. PR-52073 — San Juan, Puerto Rico
ADVERTISMENTE PARA BIDAS
The Municipal Housing Authority of the Capital of Puerto Rico will
receive sealed bids for the construction of a Housing Project located on
the "San Juan Bautista Oevelopment" (Caserio San Juan Bautista) San-
turce, Puerto Rico, y consiste en 114 Unidades de Vivienda, basura está-
tiones, and including all interior plumbing, gas instalación y electrical
work, and also outside underground utilities, all site improvements, and
the electrical distribution system, until 10:00 A. M. on the 31st day of
October, 1945, at their office in San Juan, Puerto Rico, at which time
y lugar a todos los ofertas se abrirán y se leerán a gritos.
The successful bidder will be required to furnish satisfactory per-
formance y pago bond o bonos.
Atención se llama a la realidad de que no menos de la mínima salario
rates as set forth in the specifications must be paid on this project.
Proposed forms of contract documents, including plans and specifi-
cations, are on file at the office of the Municipal Housing Authority of Madrid.
La capital de Puerto Rico.
Copias de documentos pueden obtenerse depositando treinta dólares
(830.00) con la Autoridad Municipal de Vivienda de la Capital de Puerto Rico
Rico, para cada conjunto de documentos obtenidos. El monto del depósito
will be refunded to each person who returns the plans and documents in
good condiciones dentro de 15 días después de la apertura de las ofertas.
The Municipal Housing Authority of the Capital of Puerto Rico re-
serves te right to reject any or all bids and to waive any informalities in
bidiend
A certificado check o billetera draft pagable a la Municipalidad de Vivienda
Authority of the Capital of Puerto Rico, U. S. Government Bonds, or a
satisfactorio bid bond ejecutado por el postor y una compañía de seguros, en
an amount equal to five per cent (5) of the bid shall be submitted
con cada oferta.
No bid shall be withdrawn for a period of thirty (30) días subse-
quente to the opening of bids without the consent of the Municipal House-
ing Authority of the Capital of Puerto Rico.
